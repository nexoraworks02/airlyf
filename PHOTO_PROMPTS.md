# Airlyf — photo prompts for ChatGPT

All site photos are currently shared with Fresh Air Quality. Generate new ones
with the prompts below, save each with the **exact filename**, and send them
over — they get converted to `.webp` and dropped into `public/images/photos/`.

## Paste this style block at the start of EVERY prompt

```
Photorealistic, natural-light photo for a Canadian home-service company website.
Brand look: technicians wear plain charcoal-grey work shirts and caps with a small
plain red chest patch — NO logos, NO text, NO company names anywhere (shirts, caps,
vans, equipment, walls). Bright, clean, modern Canadian homes; warm neutral walls;
a touch of red somewhere in the scene. Realistic HVAC equipment (flexible grey duct
hose, rotary duct brushes, a portable negative-air machine). No watermarks.
```

## Tips

- Generate 2–3 versions of each and pick the most natural one.
- If a shirt shows letters or a logo, ask: *"Same image, but remove all text and logos from the clothing."*
- Real photos of your own crew and jobs always beat AI images — swap them in whenever you have them.
- The 8 landmark photos in `public/images/areas/` (Niagara Falls, Moraine Lake, …) are generic and fine to keep, but are also shared with Fresh Air; replace them last.
